07 // FAQ

      
FREQUENT QUERIES

      
Everything you need before you plug in.

    


    

      

        Who can participate in Techfest 3.0?
        
Any student — undergrad, postgrad or school — can register. Industry professionals, alumni and international guests join as mentors, judges and speakers. Most competitive tracks require a valid student ID at check-in.

      

      

        Do I need to be part of a team to enter?
        
Individual entries are welcome for talks, workshops and exhibitions. Hacker-style tracks run as teams of two to four, and our matchmaking board pairs solo registrants within the first week.

      

      

        Is there a registration fee?
        
Student passes are free for all IIT Bombay students and ₹499 for others, covering every track, all meals and the closing showcase. Workshop seats are limited and released in a second wave.

      

      

        What hardware or software should I bring?
        
Bring a laptop, a charger and your own tools. Loaner hardware — microcontrollers, sensor kits, VR headsets and FPGA boards — is available at the issue desk on a first-come basis. Robotics teams should declare their chassis requirements by 5 February.

      

      

        Where does the festival happen?
        
Across the IIT Bombay campus in Powai — the Main Stage in the Convocation Hall, the Build Floor in the SAC arena, the Arena in the quadruped shed, and workshops in the lab complexes. The venue is fully wheelchair accessible.

      

      

        Will sessions be recorded or streamed?
        
Keynotes and the closing ceremony stream live to our channels. Recorded talks land on the open archive within two weeks, under a Creative Commons licence. Workshops stay in-person only.
